Getting the mods working
========================
For "Forza mods 2026-11-15.zip". About five minutes.

What you need first
  1. The Forza Motorsport Remastered folder (the PC port), already able to start the plain game with "Play Forza.bat".
  2. Your own copy of the Forza Motorsport (Xbox, 2005) disc, extracted to a folder, so that you have its default.xbe.
     The mods do not include the game.

Steps
  1. Close the game if it is running.
  2. Unzip "Forza mods 2026-11-15.zip" straight into the Forza Motorsport Remastered folder and let it merge.
     Afterwards you should have:
         Forza Motorsport Remastered\Play Forza (New mods).bat
         Forza Motorsport Remastered\runtime\forza-next.exe
         Forza Motorsport Remastered\run\mods_next\        (the mods themselves)
     Nothing of the plain game is replaced: forza.exe, your other mods folders and your saves stay as they are.
  3. Only on a PC other than the one the zip was made on: open "Play Forza (New mods).bat" in Notepad. Its last long
     line ends with the full path of default.xbe in quotes. Change that path to where your default.xbe is. Save.
  4. Double-click "Play Forza (New mods).bat".

Checking it worked
  - Arcade > the track list: Fuji Speedway and the Pacific Shipyards Circuit (and its reverse) are there, each with a
    drive-through preview.
  - Career > Buy Cars: the add-on cars are in the dealers (47 of them, from the Nissan 240SX to the Bugatti Chiron).
  - Career > Go Race: Mayhem, Drift, Time Trial and the other added series are in the Race Type list.

Saves
  The launcher uses the save disk in run\hdd_test (the "Debug" profile). To play on a different one, change the
  "set XBRT_HDD=" line in the .bat to that folder. Back the save folder up before the first run with mods: a profile
  that owns add-on cars needs those cars' files to stay as they are, so do not go back to an older mods folder on the
  same save.

If something goes wrong
  - "There's a problem with the disc you're using": the default.xbe path in the .bat is wrong, or the zip was unzipped
    one folder too deep (look for run\mods_next\media directly under the game folder).
  - The window closes at once: the .bat pauses on an error; the game's own report is in run\screenshots\play_log.txt.
  - No new tracks or cars: you started a different .bat. Only "Play Forza (New mods).bat" uses this mods folder.
  - To go back to the plain game: start "Play Forza.bat". To remove the mods: delete run\mods_next,
    runtime\forza-next.exe and "Play Forza (New mods).bat".

Good to know
  - F12 saves a screenshot to run\screenshots.
  - Fuji Speedway's pit lane can be driven through, but has no speed limit or pit camera yet.
  - README.txt in the zip lists what was tested.
