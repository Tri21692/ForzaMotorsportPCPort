Forza Motorsport mods, 15 November 2026
=======================================

What is here
  run/mods_next/              the mods folder: Fuji Speedway, the Pacific Shipyards Circuit (both ways), the 47 add-on
                              cars, Mayhem, Drift, Catch-Up, Time Trial and the other career additions, the dealer
                              backgrounds, the preview clips, pitzones.txt (the pit lanes for the Mayhem rules)
  runtime/forza-next.exe      the game build (15 Nov: the 11 Nov build the mods were checked on, plus Kaizo Mode)
  Play Forza (New mods).bat   starts the game with this mods folder

To use
  Unzip over the project folder (E:\Forza Motorsport Remastered) so the paths line up, then run the .bat.
  The .bat names the disc image's default.xbe by its full path: change that line on another PC.
  Saves are not in the zip and are not touched.

What was checked (overnight, 11-12 Nov, on forza-next's build, sound off, on a copy of the save disk)
  Tracks   one Arcade lap on the autopilot on each. Fuji Speedway, the Pacific Shipyards Circuit and its reverse:
           lap counted, no errors, 55-60 fps at worst, their preview clips play. The disc's 17 tracks all load and
           drive at 59-60 fps (the long ones and the point-to-points ran out of the 150 s before the line; on Alpine
           Ring the autopilot parked itself on the pit wall).
  Cars     all 47 add-on cars shown in the dealer.
  Career   the first series of each Race Type started from the menus and raced, Mayhem's first two both as Mayhem
           races. Several second series could not be entered: the test profile owns no car of their class.
  Archives Cars.ca2, Mega.ca2, MegaXPR.ca2 read back whole.

Since the audit (15 Nov, seen in the dealer only, not raced)
  The 32 newer add-on cars wear their own rims in place of their template cars' (the Lamborghinis had Ferrari's).
  The 1991 240SX (S13) rebuilt: smooth shading, its tail lamps and badge.

Kaizo Mode (15 Nov)
  F1 (or Back + Start) > the Kaizo Mode tab. On: you start last; a hit, leaving the road or damage ends the race
  at once, last and DNF; a clean finish pays the race's winnings twice. Not in Mayhem races or drift events.
  Checked on test runs: the tab, the last-place start, a race ended by leaving the road, the doubled payout.

Known and open
  Fuji's pit lane: the game drives the car through and lets go at the exit, and nothing respawns it there. Against
  Road Atlanta it still holds the car about 4 s at the entrance, sets no speed limit and shows no pit camera.
  Stops in a pit stall and the AI's pitting are untested on any track.
