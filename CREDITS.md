# Credits: add-on cars

The add-on cars in this project's mods folder are converted from car mods made for other games
by other people. Their bodies, textures and much of the work in them are theirs. The credits
below are as each mod's own readme gives them. Where a readme names no author, the mod is
credited by its source.

The converters, the game patches and the Forza data for these cars are this project's
(`tools/nfsu2forza.py`, `tools/cartoolkit.py`, `tools/addcar.py` and others).

## Cars from the Need for Speed games themselves

- **Chevrolet Cobalt SS**, **Lamborghini Gallardo**, **BMW M3 GTR (Most Wanted)**: from
  *Need for Speed: Most Wanted* (2005). Models and textures by EA Black Box.
- **Nissan 240SX (S13)**: from *Need for Speed: Underground* (2003). Model and textures by EA
  Black Box.
